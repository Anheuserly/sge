ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACT

Comprehensive MEP / Fire Protection Maintenance

COMPREHENSIVE • ANNUAL
Purpose: Preventive maintenance, inspection, cleaning, testing, observation and rectification of covered systems, with replacement of approved consumable/defective components included only to the extent expressly agreed in the final commercial schedule.
PROPOSED SCOPE OF COVERAGE
  • Inspection and cleaning of incoming and outgoing feeders, bus bars and relevant panel equipment.
  • Checking and cleaning of MCCB, ELCB, RCCB and ACCB.
  • Insulation testing of incoming and outgoing feeders through megger.
  • LT cable testing through hipot where required and technically applicable.
  • Checking LT panel body, foundation bolts and general physical condition.
  • Hipot / megger testing of internal bus bars where required.
  • Checking tripping and closing operation of HT panel.
  • Power transformer checks including oil testing, winding testing, Buchholz relay and LT/HT terminals, as applicable.
  • Inspection and cleaning of outgoing LT cables; checking thimble, phase sequence (RYB) and connections.
  • Checking integration with the main panel.
  • Fire pumps, valves, fire panel and accessories: inspection and reporting of damaged/defective materials.
  • Fire alarm system, detectors, MCP, hooters, wiring and panel inspection.
  • HVAC machinery and associated pumps, valves, NRV, chiller plant, AHU, condenser and accessories.
  • Defects and unserviceable equipment to be reported to the responsible client engineer.
  • Maintenance tools such as hipot, megger, test lamp and earth leakage tester within contractor scope; ladders by client where required.
SERVICE MODEL
  1. Planned preventive maintenance visits as agreed in the AMC schedule.
  2. Breakdown/complaint response for covered equipment during the contract period.
  3. Maintenance observations and recommendations recorded for client reference.
  4. Any work requiring major shutdown, specialist testing or access equipment to be coordinated in advance.
