ANNUAL MAINTENANCE CONTRACT

Non-Comprehensive Maintenance Services

LABOUR + SERVICE • MATERIAL EXTRA
Commercial principle: Routine inspection, cleaning, testing and maintenance service is covered under AMC. Replacement of equipment, components and materials is excluded from the AMC fee and shall be charged extra after client approval.
PROPOSED SCOPE OF WORK
  • Complete checking, cleaning and observation of covered equipment.
  • Checking MCCB, ELCB, RCCB and ACCB.
  • Megger testing of incoming and outgoing feeders.
  • Hipot testing of connected LT cables where required, with report/observation.
  • Checking LT panel body and foundation bolts.
  • Bus-bar testing by hipot / megger where required.
  • Checking HT panel tripping and closing.
  • Transformer checks as agreed: oil, winding, Buchholz relay and LT/HT terminals.
  • Outgoing cable inspection, thimble and RYB phase sequence checking.
  • Checking integration with the main panel.
  • Fire pumps, valves, fire panel and accessories inspection.
  • Fire alarm system, detectors, MCP, hooters, wiring and panel inspection.
  • HVAC pumps, valves, NRV, chiller plant, AHU, condenser and associated accessories inspection.
  • Defects/unserviceable equipment reported to the client's responsible engineer.
INCLUDED
  1. Routine maintenance labour and service activities listed in the approved scope.
  2. Inspection, cleaning, testing and operational checks as applicable.
  3. Standard maintenance tools within contractor scope, subject to site conditions.
  4. Maintenance observations and defect reporting.
CHARGED EXTRA
  1. Replacement of any equipment or component.
  2. Major repair and rectification requiring materials.
  3. Specialist testing or third-party certification where required.
  4. Civil, fabrication, scaffolding/access equipment and major shutdown-related work.
  5. Any item outside the signed AMC scope.
