PROPOSED SCOPE OF WORK
  • Inspection and functional testing of the existing conventional fire alarm system, including panel, zones and connected field devices.
  • Inspection and functional testing of the existing addressable fire alarm system, including panel/loop communication and connected field devices.
  • Testing and cleaning of smoke detectors, MCPs and hooters; checking physical condition and mounting.
  • Checking detector operation, alarm indication, fault indication, zone/loop status and panel annunciation.
  • Checking accessible fire alarm wiring, terminations, continuity and visible cable condition.
  • Testing manual call points and audible alarm operation at approved test points.
  • Checking batteries, charger operation, battery terminals and panel power-supply indications.
  • Checking panel event/fault history and recording abnormal observations.
  • Cleaning accessible detector heads and fire alarm panel surfaces/interiors where appropriate.
  • Identification and reporting of defective, damaged, missing or non-operational devices.
  • Post-maintenance functional testing with observations and recommendations recorded.
  • Breakdown/complaint response for covered fire alarm equipment during the AMC period, as agreed.
  • Routine replacement of covered defective/failed components within the agreed AMC scope, subject to availability and approved equivalent specifications.
  • Minor service rectification related to covered devices and terminations where it does not constitute major rewiring or capital work.
INCLUDED UNDER COMPREHENSIVE AMC
  1. Scheduled preventive maintenance and service labour.
  2. Cleaning, inspection and functional testing of conventional and addressable systems.
  3. Routine covered replacement components/materials within the approved AMC scope.
  4. Minor service rectification of covered devices and terminations.
  5. Maintenance records, defect reporting and recommendations.
EXCLUSIONS / EXTRA WORK
  1. Complete panel replacement or major system modernization.
  2. Major concealed cable replacement, extensive rewiring or major loop/zone reconfiguration.
  3. Civil work, scaffolding, special access equipment and third-party certification.
  4. Damage due to external works, misuse, unauthorized modification or force majeure.
  5. Any item outside the approved fire alarm AMC scope.
